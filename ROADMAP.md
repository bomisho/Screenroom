# Screenroom roadmap

Subtitle idea: "Movie theater for Plex". Positioning: win on the theater experience, not on being yet another Plex browser.

## Build order
1. Sign-in, browse, basic playback, tested on the headset  ← code written, awaiting first device build
2. Player polish: quiet dark controls, large timeline with scrub thumbnails (Plex preview/BIF), Skip Intro / Skip Credits (Plex markers), audio/subtitle tracks, next episode, plus a big Continue Watching card at launch that resumes straight into the last-used theater
3. One excellent theater: classic cinema (curtains, seats), with front / middle / back seat positions and subtitles legible on the screen at an adjustable size
4. App Store submission, v1.0 (the user is already enrolled in the Apple Developer Program; check name availability in App Store Connect)
5. Updates: drive-in under the stars, black void, cozy living room; **screen light** (room tinted by the video's average colors, the signature feature); stereoscopic 3D (side-by-side, MV-HEVC); SharePlay watch-together

## Monetization (to be confirmed, not hard-wired yet)
Free download with Window mode + one theater; one-time "Unlock all theaters" in-app purchase via StoreKit 2. No subscriptions.

## App Review
Create a free Plex demo account with a few public-domain movies on a small server, and put its login in the review notes (APP_STORE.md).
